The standard

How we hold
the quality line.

A

Research first

Every design starts from researched references in the client's industry — never from a generic template's defaults.

B

Screenshot QA

Builds are rendered and read at phone, tablet and desktop widths before they are scored. No rendered proof, no quality claim.

C

Honest scoring

Each build gets a written scorecard verdict against a 100-point standard — including what is missing. Especially what is missing.

D

Rising ladder

Every portfolio build must beat the previous one. A repeated defect is treated as a failure of the system, not of one page.

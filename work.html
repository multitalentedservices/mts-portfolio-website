The Gilded Basket

E-Commerce Demo — gift-basket storefront

The brief

A gift store lives or dies on the basket: browsing, building a custom basket and checking out had to feel like one short, certain flow — on a phone first.

What was built

A full storefront demo — shop, product pages, a basket builder with live totals, cart and checkout — hand-coded, with real photography and a tested order flow.

The outcome

A visitor can go from the homepage to a completed demo checkout without a dead end — the whole journey is open in the repository, labelled DEMO throughout.

Saffron House

Restaurant Website Demo

The brief

A restaurant site has one conversion spine — the booking. The menu had to read like print, and ordering and reservations had to stay one tap away in every section.

What was built

A printed-menu editorial site — menu filtering and search, WhatsApp ordering, and a reservations flow — built and tested as one connected journey.

The outcome

Menu, order and reservation paths all complete in the demo, each reachable from the homepage — open in the repository, labelled DEMO throughout.
